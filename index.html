The science, stated honestly

Specificity is the ambition.
Validation is the gate.

Bacteriophages are naturally occurring viruses that interact with bacteria. Their host specificity creates a path to investigate targeted microbiome support rather than treating every microbial population the same way.

Sulveda is being developed to translate this emerging area into a practical consumer ritual. The final product must still establish identity, activity, stability, delivery, shelf life, safety, and evidence appropriate to every public claim.

The honest answers

Before you join.

Can I buy Sulveda Daily Gut Mints today?

Not yet. Daily Gut Mints are a product-development concept. Founding access is for progress updates, research participation, and first access if a validated product advances to launch.

Is this gum, candy, or a nicotine pouch?

No. The lead concept is a swallowed, fast-dissolving dietary supplement tablet. It is being designed to feel as easy and portable as a mint without nicotine, tobacco, or a pouch that is discarded.

What will be inside?

Sulveda is evaluating targeted bacteriophage and complementary microbiome-support strategies. No final formula, ingredient list, dose, or finished-product claim has been approved.

Why is there no price yet?

The commercial offer should follow validated formulation, shelf life, manufacturing cost, retail economics, fulfillment, and leadership approval. Sulveda will not use a placeholder price as a public promise.
