The idea in plain English

Different bacteria.
A more targeted approach.

Bacteriophages, often called phages, are naturally occurring viruses that infect specific bacteria. That specificity is why Sulveda is investigating them for microbiome support.

The aim is to influence selected bacteria while supporting a balanced microbial community. The final blend and delivery need to be validated in the finished mint.

What is Sulveda intended to help with?

Sulveda’s development goals are digestive wellness and microbiome support. Its approach explores selected bacterial populations, beneficial organisms, and normal function. The finished mint formula and product-specific benefits are still being validated.

Has Sulveda been shown to heal the gut?

No finished-product evidence has been supplied establishing that these mints heal the gut, reduce inflammation, or treat digestive conditions. Sulveda is being developed as a nutritional wellness product, with claims that must match the evidence.

Is it a probiotic mint?

The formula is not final. Sulveda is evaluating targeted phages and potentially complementary prebiotic, probiotic, or other nutritional ingredients. The full ingredient list and Supplement Facts will be published once approved.

Yes. The shop lets you choose a supply and prepare an order inquiry. Availability, payment, shipping, and final terms still need to be confirmed before purchase.
