Orders & availability

How do I place an order?

Choose a supply in the shop and add it to your bag. The checkout prepares an enquiry you can review, copy, download, or send through your email app. The website does not submit an order automatically or collect payment.

When will it ship?

A launch date and fulfillment window have not been confirmed. Your enquiry does not reserve inventory. Ask the team about product availability, delivery times, shipping costs, and return terms before any payment.

Are the prices final?

The current proposed launch prices are $29.99 for one dispenser, $24.99 per dispenser every 30 days for monthly delivery, and $69.99 for three dispensers. Final prices, tax, shipping, and availability must be confirmed before purchase.

Monthly delivery

The proposed monthly plan sends one 60-count dispenser every 30 days at $24.99 per dispenser, before any confirmed tax or shipping. Additional quantities multiply that proposed recurring amount.

Choosing this option or preparing an enquiry does not start automatic billing. Renewal consent, payment, and the process for skipping, pausing, or canceling must be confirmed before a paid subscription begins.

Product information

Sulveda Daily Gut Mints are a developing supplement concept: 60 Cool Spearmint mints per pocket dispenser, with a planned two-mint daily serving.

The complete ingredient list, Supplement Facts, allergens, storage requirements, and final directions will be provided once approved. Product illustrations are packaging concepts. Health outcomes and finished-product efficacy have not been established by this website.

How this preview handles information

The bag saves product choices and quantities in this browser's local storage so they can stay with you between pages. It does not save your name, email, phone, postal code, or notes.

Contact information entered on the enquiry page stays in that page until you choose to open an email, copy, or download it. Opening an email prepares a draft addressed to williamcolton@paldara.com; you complete sending in your email app. Downloaded details may include the contact information you entered.

GitHub Pages hosts this preview and Google Fonts supplies its typefaces; those providers receive the network requests needed to serve the site. This site does not include advertising pixels or an analytics script.

For questions about an emailed enquiry or to request deletion of information you sent, email williamcolton@paldara.com.
